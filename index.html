Sum &
Product Sprint

Find two numbers that add to the given sum and multiply to the given product.

How to play:

The game will show you two clues: ADD TO GIVE and MULTIPLY TO GIVE. Choose one number from each row, then press Submit.

The order does not matter. For example, choosing 3 then 4 is the same as choosing 4 then 3.

You have 2 minutes. Two correct answers in a row moves you up a level. Two incorrect answers in a row moves you down a level.
